Point Gomoku — Official Rules

Featured in The Devil's Plan: Dead Again (Season 3, Episode 13) as the final Exile Zone duel, Point Gomoku combines five-in-a-row tactics with simultaneous point-bidding and resource hoarding.

  1. Board & Goal: Played on a 15×15 Gomoku grid. Be the first player to connect 5 stones in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  2. Phase 1 — Selection (90s): Players alternate as the Selector each round (Black starts in Round 1). The Selector picks 3 consecutive empty intersections in a straight line (Spots A, B, and C).
  3. Phase 2 — Secret Bidding: Both players receive 10 points each round. Simultaneously and secretly, allocate whole points across Spots A, B, and C (minimum 1 point per spot).
  4. Carry-Over Rule: You do not have to spend all your points! Half of your unspent points (rounded down) carry over and are added to next round's 10 points. For example, saving 7 points grants +3 carry-over, giving you 13 points next round.
  5. Phase 3 — Placement & Tie-Breaker: For each of the 3 spots, the higher bidder places their stone there. If both players bid the exact same amount on a spot, the non-selector wins the tie.
  6. Fair P2P Commit-Reveal: In online Puter.js matches, your bet is sealed with a SHA-256 hash and only revealed once both peers have locked in, preventing any peeking.
